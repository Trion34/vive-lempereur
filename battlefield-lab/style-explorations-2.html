Round 2 — Refinements of Period Map (#4) + Engraving (#6)

A. Symbology + Hatch Hybrid

#4's clean layout (facing line, flag, unit text) with #6's diagonal hatch fill. The natural fusion of both.

B. Visible Rank Structure

Period map blocks with thin internal lines showing each rank. You can see the 3-deep structure of the line and the massed depth of the column.

C. Copper Plate Print

Refined engraving with varied line weights — thicker at edges, thinner inside. Warm cream tones on dark ground. Like a real period print.

D. Silhouette + Bright Facing

Formations as solid dark masses with a bright glowing front edge. High contrast, instantly readable. The "wall of men" feel.

E. Ink Wash Battle Plan

Gradient fill — darker at the facing edge, fading toward the rear. Like a watercolour wash on a period battle map. Soft but structured.

F. Etched Emboss

Engraving-style hatch with a raised embossed effect — light edge on top, shadow on bottom. Like a stamped metal battle medal.
