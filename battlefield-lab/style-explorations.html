Formation Art Style Explorations — French Line (3 ranks, ~120 files)

1. Solid Block + Texture

One filled shape per formation. Cross-hatching inside differentiates unit type. Period battle-map engraving feel.

2. Top-Down Miniatures

Circle-on-rectangle shapes (head + shoulders from above). Figurative — reads as people on a wargame table.

3. Line-and-Bristle

Formation outline with short perpendicular tick marks along the front edge representing bayonets. Density = strength. Minimal and elegant.

4. Period Map Symbology

Solid colored rectangles with facing line and flag marker. How actual 1790s battle maps depicted units. Clean and historically grounded.

5. Stipple Cloud

No grid. Random scatter of dots within a formation boundary. Density communicates strength. Organic and painterly.

6. Engraving Style

Parallel lines at varying density inside formation outlines. Like a copper-plate engraving. Black and cream — deeply thematic for the period.
