# Gallica/BnF & Institutional Art Catalogue — The Little Soldier

Public domain Napoleonic art from French national library (Gallica/BnF), Library of Congress, and institutional collections for the Italian Campaign 1796-97 game.

**Research date**: 2026-03-15

---

## TIER 1 — Must-Use Sources

### Tableaux historiques des campagnes d'Italie (Complete Engraving Series)
- **Artists:** Carle Vernet (designs), Jean Duplessi-Bertaux (etching), Delignon/Masquelier/Bovinet/Peronard (engraving)
- **Date:** 1799-1806
- **THE definitive visual record of the Italian Campaign** — ~24 engraved plates covering every major battle
- **URLs:**
  - BnF catalog: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb402506413
  - French Military Digital Library: https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/centre-culturel-militaire-de-lyon/document/d0f58279-f27f-4139-990e-e0b41f41d46c
  - Individual Rivoli plate at Met: https://metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/894622
  - Arcole plate at BnF: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb402543119
  - Fondation Napoleon Rivoli: https://collections.fondationnapoleon.org/document/bataille-de-rivoli-les-25-et-26-nivose-an-5/627ccf193b7ac923bd62ad88
- **License:** Public domain
- **Chapters:** ALL — covers every battle in the game
- **Usefulness:** HIGH — Period-authentic engravings, the single most important art source

### West Point Digital History Center — Napoleonic Wars Atlas
- **Professional military campaign and battle maps**, 300 dpi JPEGs
- **URLs:**

  - Northern Italy 1796 (Interior Lines): https://wpvip.westpoint.edu/dhc/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/01/Nap03-01.jpg

## TIER 2 — High-Value Finds

### Le Soldat Français (Charlet) — Cleveland Museum
- **Date:** 1818, lithograph
- **URL:** https://www.clevelandart.org/art/2009.519
- **License:** Public domain / Open Access — "copy, modify, distribute without restrictions"
- **Description:** Wounded French soldier victoriously reloading. Quintessential soldier image.
- **Usefulness:** HIGH — Perfect player character reference

### Bivouac at Austerlitz (Swebach) — MFA Boston
- **URL:** https://collections.mfa.org/objects/247749/napoleonic-campaigns-bivouac-at-austerlitz
- **Description:** Soldiers around campfire, cooking and resting. Brown wash drawing.
- **Usefulness:** HIGH — Direct camp phase reference

### Detaille Napoleonic Uniform Studies (Wikimedia)
- **37 public domain files** — the gold standard for uniform accuracy
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:%C3%89douard_Detaille-Napoleonic_Wars
- **Usefulness:** HIGH

### Nicolas Hoffmann Military Uniform Watercolors
- **French army uniforms, Revolutionary period** — hand-colored engravings
- **URLs:**
  - Brown University: https://repository.library.brown.edu/studio/collections/id_619/
  - Brown library: https://library.brown.edu/cds/askb/
- **License:** Public domain
- **Usefulness:** HIGH — Period-specific (1790s)

### Anne S.K. Brown Military Collection (Brown University)
- **15,000+ military prints, drawings, watercolors** — "foremost American collection"
- **URLs:**
  - Digital: https://repository.library.brown.edu/studio/collections/id_619/
  - Overview: https://library.brown.edu/info/collections/askb/index/
- **Usefulness:** HIGH — Premier reference collection

## KNOWN GAP — Bagetti Watercolors

Giuseppe Pietro Bagetti painted nearly every Italian Campaign battlefield in watercolor. Held at the Royal Library of Turin (Biblioteca Reale). **Not significantly digitized online.** Would be the most historically accurate battlefield depictions if accessible. The Royal Library portal: https://museireali.beniculturali.it/biblioteca-reale/
