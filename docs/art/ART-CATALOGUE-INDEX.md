## Top 10 Must-Download Images

1. **Philippoteaux — Napoleon at Rivoli** — the game's central battle
2. **Gros — Bonaparte at Arcole** — iconic young Napoleon, title screen material
3. **Vernet/Duplessi-Bertaux — Bataille de Rivoli engraving** — period-authentic, high-res
4. **Detaille — Bonaparte in Italy 1797** — gold standard uniform accuracy
5. **Charlet — Le Soldat Français** — quintessential player character reference
6. **Swebach — Bivouac at Austerlitz** — camp phase atmosphere
7. **West Point — Rivoli tactical map** — accurate battle geography
8. **West Point — Northern Italy campaign map** — for the Tier 1 animated SVG map
9. **Lejeune — Passage du Pont de Lodi** — dramatic bridge charge composition
10. **Bagetti — Voltri watercolor** — the game's opening location

## Pipeline

1. Browse catalogues → pick images by chapter need
2. Download originals to `public/art-reference/{category}/`
3. Run through NanoBanana 2 with consistent style prompt for game-ready versions
4. Store styled versions in `public/assets/art/`

## Known Gaps

- **Ch.9 Caldiero** — no battle paintings found (Napoleon's defeat, less celebrated)
- **Alpine/Italian landscapes** — few pure landscape images; most are battle scenes with landscape backgrounds
- **Austrian commanders** — Alvintzi and Wurmser portraits not found
- **Bagetti watercolors** — the best source for Italian Campaign battlefields, but not digitized online (held at Royal Library of Turin)
- **Italian peasant women** — very few public domain paintings of ordinary Northern Italian women from the 1790s
- **Actual 1790s vivandiere images** — most vivandiere art is 1820s-1860s retrospective; no confirmed 1796-97 era depiction found
