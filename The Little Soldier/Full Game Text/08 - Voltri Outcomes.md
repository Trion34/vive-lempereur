# Voltri Outcomes
*Battle of Voltri — End States*

---

## Survived: Dawn at Savona

> You made it. Forty kilometres of coastal road, stumbling through the dark, and you made it.
>
> The garrison at Savona opens its gates as the first grey light touches the sea. Men collapse where they stand — in doorways, against walls, in the middle of the road. Officers stop trying to maintain order. There is no order. There is only exhaustion.
>
> Voltri is lost. The supplies, the position, the illusion of safety — all gone. But the 14th is intact. Battered, footsore, half-dead with fatigue, but intact.
>
> Somewhere to the north, in the mountains, Bonaparte is planning something. The campaign has not even begun.

---

## Defeat (Melee): Killed in Action

> The steel finds you on the heights above Voltri. A brief, ugly skirmish in the olive groves — and then nothing.
>
> Your war ends on a sunny April afternoon, within sight of the sea, before the campaign has even begun. The garrison retreats without you.
>
> The 10th of April, 1796. This is where your war ends.

---

## Defeat (Line): Killed in Action

> The ball finds you during the exchange on the Pegli heights. No time to duck, no warning — just the impact and the fall.
>
> The company fires on without you. The retreat sounds an hour later. By then, it no longer matters.
>
> The 10th of April, 1796. This is where your war ends.

---

## Rout: Broke

> You ran before the withdrawal was ordered. Dropped your musket somewhere on the heights and scrambled down toward the coast road, legs moving before your mind could catch up.
>
> You reach Savona with the stragglers, hours after the main body. Sergeant Morin finds you. He says nothing. The look is enough.
>
> You survived Voltri. That word will taste like ashes.
