## Narrative Chunks

### Chunk 1

> The Army of Italy starves on the Riviera. Stretched thin from Nice to Genoa, the soldiers of the Republic endure in wretched form. The Directory believes Italy is a sideshow -- the real war is on the Rhine.

### Chunk 2

> A new general has taken command. Young and blazing with ambition. The men don't know what to make of him yet.

### Chunk 3

> You are a private soldier garrisoned at Voltri -- a fishing town on the Ligurian coast, seventeen kilometers west of Genoa. A strategic position between mountains and sea. The Austrian army gathers somewhere in those mountains.

### Chunk 4

> You are young, untested and already beleaguered by the sorry state of French Army of Italy. You are no one special.
