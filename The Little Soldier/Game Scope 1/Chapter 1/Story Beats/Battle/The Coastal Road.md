# The Coastal Road

**Type:** Story beat (chargeEncounter=12, VoltriCoastalRoad)
**Trigger:** After The Line Breaks

---

## Narrative

> Night falls on the Ligurian coast. The column staggers west along the corniche road — a narrow track carved between the mountains and the sea.
>
> No lights. No drums. The officers have forbidden both. The Austrians are somewhere behind, and nobody wants to find out how close.
>
> Men stumble in the dark. Equipment clatters. Someone falls and curses. The road is rutted, broken, treacherous in the blackness.
>
> Ahead of you, a soldier has collapsed at the roadside. His pack is still on. He might be sleeping. He might be done.

## Choices

### Help the straggler
*Haul him up. No one gets left behind on this road. [Constitution check]*

**Success:**
> You grab the man's arm and haul him up. He's heavy — dead weight at first, then his legs find the rhythm. [Constitution: passed]
>
> "Merci," he mumbles. You don't know his name. You don't need to. You half-carry him for the next mile until he can manage on his own.
>
> Sergeant Morin sees it. Says nothing. But he remembers.

- soldierRep +2
- Stamina -10

**Failure:**
> You try to haul him up, but your own legs are giving out. [Constitution: failed]
>
> The effort costs you. Your calves cramp. You stagger, nearly go down yourself. Another soldier takes over. The straggler gets moving, but you're in worse shape than before.

- Stamina -20

### Keep moving
*If you stop, you might not start again. Someone else will help him.*

> You step around the fallen man. Don't look down. Don't stop. If you stop, you'll be the next one sitting at the roadside.
>
> The column grinds on. Behind you, someone else helps the straggler. Or doesn't. You don't look back.

- Morale -2: "Left a comrade behind — necessary but cold"

## Mechanics
- Constitution check at standard difficulty
- No health cost — this beat is about stamina and moral choice
- Transitions to Cavalry Scare (chargeEncounter=13)
