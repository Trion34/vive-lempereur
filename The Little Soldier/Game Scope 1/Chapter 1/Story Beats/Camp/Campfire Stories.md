## Narrative

> Evening. Your section gathers around a driftwood fire on the shingle beach. The salt air carries the smell of smoke and someone's thin soup. One of the veterans is talking — not about the war, but about the Revolution. The September days. The prisons. The crowds. The younger men listen with wide eyes. The others are quiet.

## Choices

### Join in
*Add your own story. Draw the men closer. [Charisma check]*

**Success:**
> You tell them about your village. The harvest festival, the church bell, the dog that followed you to the recruiting station. It's not a war story — it's better. Someone laughs for the first time in days. The veteran nods. "That's what we're fighting for," he says. "Not liberty. Not glory. Home."

- soldierRep +2, morale +3

**Failure:**
> You start to speak but the words come out wrong — stilted, forced. The men listen politely and turn back to the veteran. You stare into the fire and wish you'd kept your mouth shut.

- morale -1

### Stay quiet
*Listen. Let the fire and the stories do their work.*

> You listen. The veteran talks about the old days, the good days, before the war turned into this endless hungry march. Someone asks questions. The fire crackles. For a while, just for a while, you are not soldiers — just men, far from home, sharing warmth.

- morale +2
