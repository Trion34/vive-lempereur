## Narrative

> At dusk, someone spots them — thin columns of smoke rising from the mountain passes to the north. Campfires. Austrian campfires. Sergeant Morin squints up at the ridgeline and shrugs. "Scouts, maybe. Or shepherds. Nothing to worry about." But he checks the priming on his musket as he says it.

## Choices

### Stay calm
*Keep your nerve. It's probably nothing. [Valor check]*

**Success:**
> "It's nothing," you say, and you almost believe it. The men around you relax. Morin glances at you with something that might be approval. A steady voice matters more than a sharp eye, sometimes.

- soldierRep +1, morale +3
- Morin relationship +3

**Failure:**
> You try to stay calm but your eyes keep drifting to the ridgeline. The smoke is still there. More of it, maybe. Your hands find your musket and stay there. Sleep will not come easily tonight.

- morale -2

### Report to the lieutenant
*Pass it up the chain. Let Vidal decide. [Awareness check]*

**Success:**
> You find Lieutenant Vidal and report the fires — their number, their spacing, which passes they occupy. He listens without expression. "I'll pass it up," he says. "Good eyes, soldier." Coming from Vidal, that's high praise.

- officerRep +2, morale +2
- Vidal relationship +4

**Failure:**
> You report to Lieutenant Vidal, but you can't say how many fires there were, or where exactly. He frowns. "I need facts, not nerves. Dismissed." You leave feeling foolish.

- morale -1
- Vidal relationship -2
