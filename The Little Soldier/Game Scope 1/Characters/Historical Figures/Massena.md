## Overview
Commander of the fresh division that arrives to stabilize the battle. Never described physically — known only through the sound and impact of his division arriving. His attack is the turning point that buys time for the 14th.

## Role in Demo

### Prologue
- "Massena's division marches behind him [Bonaparte]."

### Massena's Respite (Story Beat)
- His division arrives from the south with drums, fresh troops in blue coats, bayonets glinting.
- "Massena's division slams into the Austrian flank like a fist. The columns stagger, turn, try to reform against this new threat."
- The pressure on the 14th eases for the first time since dawn.
- "Massena's attack bought time. Not victory."
- This buys five minutes of respite — the player's recovery window.

### Line Part 2
- Volley 5 (scripted): "Massena still fighting." (+3 morale)

### Player Choices
- **Massena's Respite** offers three recovery choices:
  - Tend Wounds — health +15, stamina +30
  - Check on Comrades — stamina +15, NPC morale check
  - Follow the Screaming — Intelligence check, assist surgeon

## Design Notes
- No direct dialogue in any file
- Never described physically — only through the effect of his arrival
- His arrival marks the transition from Part 1 to Part 2
- Morale contagion: +3 at Volley 5 ("Massena still fighting")
