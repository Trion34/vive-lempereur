## Overview
The section sergent. Practical, tired, fair. Keeps the men together. Veteran of Valmy — the Revolution's first battle. The constant thread of authority through Voltri's chaos.

## Appearance
- Squints at ridgelines, shrugs at threats
- Checks the priming on his musket while saying "nothing to worry about"
- Voice: whispered commands in the dark, barked orders on the hilltop
- "Already moving" when orders come — never surprised, never still
- Counting heads, checking equipment — "the sergeant's work that never ends"

## Background
- Veteran of Valmy (20 September 1792) — the Revolution's first battle
- "We stood in the rain and the Prussians turned back. I thought the war was over."
- Three or more years of campaigning since
- Professional soldier who has made his peace with the army's suffering

## Personality
- Practical — deals with what is, not what should be
- Tired but not broken — weariness without cynicism
- Fair — treats the men justly, expects competence
- Bitter humor — laughs "short and bitter" at his own naiveté about Valmy ending the war
- Grudging approval: "Good man." Two words. They mean everything.
- Observant — "Says nothing. But he remembers."

## Arc
The reliable NCO. Unlike Duval at Rivoli (whose wounding creates a crisis), Morin survives the entire battle. His value is consistency — the constant authority figure who holds the section together through retreat, darkness, and fear. His rare words of approval are the player's measure of worth.

## Relationships
- **Player**: Starts at 30. Professional respect, not warmth. Built through observed competence — he notices everything.
- **Vidal**: Chain of command. Morin executes, Vidal decides.

## Design Notes
- Equivalent to Duval at Rivoli (NCO), but does NOT get wounded — survives the whole battle
- No `ncoPresent` crisis mechanic at Voltri
- Appears in multiple withdrawal story beats as the constant voice of authority
- His approval progression: silence → "a look that might be respect" → "Good man"
- Base stats: Valor 60, Morale 90, maxMorale 100, Relationship 30
