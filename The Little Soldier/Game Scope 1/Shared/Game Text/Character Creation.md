## Glory Shop

**Glory Banner (has glory):**
> Each stat increase costs 1 Glory.

**Glory Banner (empty):**
> Earned through valorous deeds across campaigns.

### Grace Purchase
**Grace available:**
> Divine favour. Spend 5 Glory to gain 1 Grace (max 2).

**Grace full:**
> Grace is full.

**Not enough Glory:**
> Requires 5 Glory.
