This is Chapter one (The beginning of the game - and also the demo). The player is an unimportant, young garrison soldier at Voltri. Bonaparte has taken over command of the French Army of Italy. Conditions are very poor. Pay is behind. Food is scarce. Many soldiers lack even proper footwear. 

Intros with a prologue - the moves to the first camp sequence in the game.
The camp at Voltri functions as the tutorial for the camp system. The player spends time per his choices, preparing himself for what is to come. Some events fire in this time, introducing the player to basic choices and systems, like money and virtue - morale and fatigue as well. 


Events that can fire during the first camp.
- One opportunity to steal supplies along with a group of tough, morally questionable guys. 
(At least on NPC should be named, as going along can lead to other opportunities down the road)
- One opportunity to protect a pretty Ligurian girl from a seedy corporal 
(Can lead to other opportunities down the road)
- One opportunity to purchase extra vittles from a Genoese merchant
- One opportunity to write about your experience, and the setting so far (Gated by literacy)
- One opportunity to gamble 
(This opportunity leads to the steal supplies opportunity - as the player gets in with the thieves)

After the Camp the Austrians arrive. 
- Introduction to Line combat. (This will be a brief demonstration of the line combat system as you participate in the skirmish with the Austrians)
- Introduction to Melee combat (Functions also a tutorial opportunity, relatively brief and easy combat)

The withdrawal. 
You are party of the withdrawal of the garrison to join up with other French forces... some events will fire during the night march to Savona.

The road to Savona.
- During the march to Savona, the player will encounter a wounded soldier. If he stops to help him - they are separated from the main army along with a terribly injured young soldier. 
- The player either successful helps the soldier, and the soldier joins him. Or the Soldier dies, and the player chooses whether or not to loot the soldier. 
- The player then winds up at the homestead of the Ligurian girl. This is either a hostile or friendly encounter depending on prior choices.
